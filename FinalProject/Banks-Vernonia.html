Songs Along the Railroad

About this Map

This map examines the present day Banks-Vernonia State Trail through the perspective of a potential musical history. Specifically, this map provides recordings of common folk melodies that were popular in the PNW during the 1920s when the Oregon-American Lumber Company was operating a railway to accomodate the large lumber industry in the area. This map invites you to imagine what songs you may have encountered along the railway as a 1920s lumber worker on an iconic path that is now marked in the present day by trailheads and serves as a paved bike/walking trail.

Banks Trailhead

Source: The Oregon Encyclopedia, "Oregon-American Lumber Company"
In the 1920s, the logging industry in Vernonia, Oregon was booming. At the modern day Banks Trailhead, employees may have hopped on the train and ridden to the timber mill 20 miles North in Vernonia. Along the way, you may have heard this popular 1800s melody known as "Blackberry Blossom".
